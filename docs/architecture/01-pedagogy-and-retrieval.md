# Retrieval Practice Pedagogy Specification

Cadence departs from traditional rote flashcard flipping by enforcing direct active retrieval mechanisms.

## Core Pedagogical Principles

1. **Active Retrieval vs. Passive Review**:
   Merely re-reading lecture notes or passively flipping a flashcard produces an illusion of competence. Active retrieval forces the brain to reconstruct knowledge from neural pathways, reinforcing synaptic connections.

2. **Desirable Difficulty**:
   Retrieval is most effective when it requires effort. Cadence incorporates cloze deletion with typing, distractor discrimination in multiple-choice questions, and time constraints on mock exams.

3. **Interleaving and Spaced Intervals**:
   Rather than massed practice (cramming), retrieval sessions are distributed across optimal memory decay intervals computed by FSRS.
